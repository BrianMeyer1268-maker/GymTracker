Matrix catalog reference images
================================

The .webp images in this folder are per-machine crops from the publicly distributed
Matrix Strength Brochure 2021, used ONLY as private, local reference thumbnails inside
this personal, access-gated app to help identify unlabeled machines on the gym floor.

- These images are NOT app-owned content. All product images/designs are © Matrix /
  Johnson Health Tech.
- They are used here for personal identification/reference only, not redistribution.
- The app prefers a real photo you take of the actual machine; the catalog image is
  only shown as a fallback until a gym photo exists.
- The source PDF is not committed and is not exposed as a download from the app.

Regenerate with scripts/importMatrixCatalogImages.ts (see that file for instructions).
