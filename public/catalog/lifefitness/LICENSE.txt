Life Fitness catalog reference images
=====================================

The .webp images in this folder are per-machine product shots extracted from the
publicly distributed Life Fitness Product Catalog 2021, used ONLY as private, local
reference thumbnails inside this personal, access-gated app.

They stand in for the gym's cardio machines (treadmill, elliptical, bikes), which the
Matrix Strength Brochure does not cover. The named machine is a Matrix model; the
reference image is the equivalent Life Fitness machine type, shown only until a real
gym photo is taken.

- These images are NOT app-owned content. All product images/designs are © Life Fitness.
- Used here for personal identification/reference only, not redistribution.
- The app prefers a real photo you take of the actual machine; the catalog image is
  only a fallback until a gym photo exists.
- The source PDF is not committed and is not exposed as a download from the app.

Regenerate with scripts/importExtractedImages.ts (see that file for instructions).
